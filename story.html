The idea

AromaClip is a nasal micro-inhaler filled with one hundred percent natural BIO essential oils — a quiet alternative to caffeine, sprays and pills. Clip it to the nose, draw a slow breath, and let something natural do the rest. Compact enough to forget you are carrying it: at the desk, in the gym, on the road.

Three states

One clip, three blends. Breathe, for a clear, open inhale. Focus, for steady attention without caffeine. Sleep, to soften the evening into rest. Each is composed for a defined character, not a generic scent.

Made with care

Developed in Berlin with ENT specialists, pharmacologists and aromatherapists, from certified BIO oils and medical-grade silicone. Light, hypoallergenic and reusable — kept in its case between moments, ready whenever the day asks.

Not a scent for the mood, but a small instrument for the breath — carried, and returned to, as often as you need.

Our philosophy

AromaClip began in Berlin, in the quiet space between clinical practice and everyday life. Working alongside ENT specialists, we kept returning to a simple observation: the breath is the one instrument we always carry, and the one we most often forget to use.

So we made something small enough to keep in a pocket and considered enough to trust — a silicone clip, three essential blends, nothing added that the nose does not need. No propellants. No pills. Only the deliberate act of breathing well.

Breathe. Feel. Live.
